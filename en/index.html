Research focus
Child development · Reading education

Dr. Simge Filoğlu Soydan is a lecturer in the Child Development Programme at Ankara Hacı Bayram Veli University’s Polatlı Vocational School of Social Sciences. She is an instructor in the BİL-ETOM Reading Development Programme.

She studied social studies teaching and social studies education at Ahi Evran University and completed a PhD in child development at Ankara University. Her doctoral research examined a computer-assisted interactive reading intervention in relation to oral reading, comprehension, working memory and self-esteem in children with dyslexia.

Her publications cover child development, dyslexia, reading skills and values education in social studies. Her work also appears under the names Simge Soydan and Simge Filoğlu.
